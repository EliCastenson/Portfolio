01 Started winter 2025–26 In progress

Custom RC car

A ground-up RC car for a Formula RC league idea in my engineering club. Version 1 was hard to change and hard to print, so I rebuilt it with a separate file for every part and fasteners instead of press fits. After a lot of drivetrain failures, it drives. Now I'm designing a stronger motor shaft adapter.

  1. Researched what every RC car needs and ordered the electronics
  2. CAD version 1
  3. Rebuilt with separate part files and fasteners
  4. Fixed camber on all four wheels
  5. Hand-bent M2 steering rods; steering demo filmed Oct 22, 2026
  6. First drive
  7. Motor shaft adapter
  8. Formula RC league

02 Started spring 2026 In progress

3D printed pickleball paddle

A flexible TPU honeycomb core between two PETG plates, locked together by a keyed rim guard, a dovetail handle, and a heat-set insert. The first one flexed too much and the PETG-CF version split on a forehand. Glue and a fix to the insert made both playable, and they've held up in real games. Now I'm testing how TPU core thickness changes the feel.

  1. Researched how commercial paddles are built
  2. CAD: TPU core, PETG plates, keyed rim, dovetail handle
  3. Printed and assembled PETG and PETG-CF versions
  4. Fixed slipping layers and the split insert
  5. Playing in real games; slow-motion clip filmed Oct 28, 2026
  6. TPU core thickness test
  7. ABS plates and a regulation-size face

03 Started fall 2026 In progress

AP Research: cycloidal vs. planetary reducers

A comparison of student-designed, 3D printed (PLA) cycloidal and planetary speed reducers, both exactly 7:1, printed and tested the same way for efficiency, noise, and backlash. Most published studies test one design by itself, so their results can't be compared. I'm on the IRB form and method plan now.

  1. Research question and literature review
  2. IRB form and method plan
  3. Design both reducers in CAD
  4. Calibrate, print, and assemble
  5. Calibrate the test rig and run a pilot test
  6. Test efficiency, backlash, and noise
  7. Analyze in JASP and write it up
